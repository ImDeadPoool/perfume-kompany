What is the difference between 25ml, 50ml, and 100ml?

All three sizes share the exact same 35% pure oil formulation. The 25ml Pocket Extrait (₹549–₹599) is ideal for travel and trial. The 50ml Signature Flacon (₹799–₹899) is our most popular daily wear size. The 100ml Grand Collector Flacon (₹1,099–₹1,199) comes in a heavy solid crystal flacon with knurled gold crown.
