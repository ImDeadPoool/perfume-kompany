# Perfume Kompany - Interactive 3D Luxury Haute Parfumerie Boutique

An ultra-modern, interactive 3D flagship boutique experience built for **Perfume Kompany**.

---

## 🚀 How to View the Website
You don't need any complex installation or Node.js server to run it.

1. Open your File Explorer to:
   `D:\Praveen\new website\`
2. Double-click **`index.html`** (or right-click and choose **Open with Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge**).
3. The website will load instantly in your browser!

---

## ✨ Features & Upgrades

1. **Clean, Minimalist Entrance**:
   - Official 3D gold logo emblem with gold breathing halo.
   - Refined luxury typography (*"Haute Parfumerie • Pure Extrait"*).
   - Single prominent call to action: **"ENTER THE STORE"**.

2. **Full E-Commerce Shopping Bag & Cart Drawer**:
   - Live counter badge (`BAG (0)`) in the top navigation bar.
   - Slide-over luxury bag drawer showing selected flacons, bottle thumbnails, prices, and quantity controls.
   - Size selection: Toggle between **50 ML** and **100 ML** with live price calculation and real-time 3D flacon scaling.
   - Free VIP Insured Delivery and Certificate of Authenticity included.

3. **Seamless Checkout & Direct WhatsApp Ordering**:
   - Checkout modal with customer delivery details and payment preferences.
   - Instant 1-click **Direct WhatsApp Order** button that pre-formats the cart items, quantities, and customer details into a ready-to-send WhatsApp order!

4. **Enhanced 3D In-Store Experience**:
   - **Floating Prev / Next Navigation**: Chevrons on screen and Keyboard Arrow keys (`←` / `→`) smoothly glide between the 5 signature pedestals.
   - **Ambience Lighting Switcher**: Toggle between *Midnight Gold*, *Noir Gala*, and *Emerald Royale* store lighting.
   - **Real Mechanical Atomizer Plunge**: The 24K knurled gold cap physically depresses down and springs back up on every spray press.
   - **Dual-Layer Aerosol Vapor Mist**: Conical atomized vapor clouds with physical air resistance and thermal upward curl, accompanied by glistening micro-droplets.
   - **Pedestal Scent Aura**: Soft radial light pulse that flares up when spraying.
   - **Theo The Master Perfumer**: Interactive concierge scent finder questionnaire that recommends the perfect extrait and auto-navigates to its pedestal.

5. **5 True 3D Perfume Objects**:
   - Modeled with crystal glass, chamfered bevels, solid crystal base, glowing extrait liquid (*One Billion, Bleu Mist, Paradoxe, 10000 Wishes, Eterna Flora*), internal dip tube, gold bezel frame, and knurled gold cap.
   - 360° interactive orbit controls and auto turntable rotation.
